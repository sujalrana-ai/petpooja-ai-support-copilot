
TITLE: Online Order Synchronization Troubleshooting
CATEGORY: order_sync
SOURCE_TYPE: Illustrative portfolio documentation

SYMPTOM:
Online orders are not appearing in the restaurant POS.

POSSIBLE CAUSES:
- Unstable or unavailable network connection.
- Online ordering integration is disconnected.
- Synchronization events are pending or failing.
- The upstream ordering service may be unavailable.

SAFE CHECKS:
1. Check the device network connection.
2. Determine whether one device or several devices are affected.
3. Review the application's synchronization status.
4. Check integration status using authorized diagnostics.
5. Preserve pending orders and verify their status before retrying.

ESCALATION:
If the problem continues, contact authorized support with the
approximate incident time, error message, affected device and
sanitized diagnostic logs.

SAFETY:
Do not delete pending orders or repeatedly retry payments without
verifying their status.

LIMITATION:
This guide is illustrative and is not an official Petpooja procedure.
