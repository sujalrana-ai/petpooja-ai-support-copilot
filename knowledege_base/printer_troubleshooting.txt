TITLE: Receipt Printer Troubleshooting
CATEGORY: printer
SOURCE_TYPE: Illustrative portfolio documentation

SYMPTOM:
The restaurant receipt printer is not printing.

POSSIBLE CAUSES:
- Printer power or connection problem.
- Incorrect printer selection or configuration.
- Paper is missing or incorrectly loaded.
- Printer or application reports an error.

SAFE CHECKS:
1. Check power and visible printer status.
2. Check paper availability and loading.
3. Confirm the selected printer and connection status.
4. Check whether the problem affects one terminal or several.
5. Record the exact error message.

ESCALATION:
Contact authorized support if the issue persists. Include the
printer model, terminal details, error message and incident time.

SAFETY:
Do not change network security settings or factory-reset equipment
without authorization.

LIMITATION:
This guide is illustrative and is not an official Petpooja procedure.
